A platform first. Products as tenants.

The Aptiveon Workflow Engine is the underlying infrastructure. The Knowledge Assistant is the first product built on top of it. Future products are tenants of the same platform, not new codebases.

PRODUCT 02.1 · AWE

Aptiveon Workflow Engine

A configurable platform for orchestrating AI workflows, retrieval pipelines, integrations, and human-in-the-loop review.

PLATFORM IN DEVELOPMENT SELF-HOSTED OR MANAGED
SPEC SHEET
InputsDocuments, APIs, schedules, events, operators
OutputsOrchestrated tasks, retrieved context, reviewed outputs
StackTypeScript · Python · Postgres · queues
MaturityIn development · early partners
EngagementHosted by Aptiveon, or self-hosted
F.01
Workflow orchestration

Compose multi-step workflows with retries, branches, and explicit failure paths. Inspect any run.

F.02
Retrieval pipelines

Pluggable chunking, embeddings, vector + lexical retrieval, re-rankers, citation built in.

F.03
Integrations

API, database, webhook, and SaaS adapters. Typed contracts at every boundary.

F.04
Human review

Drop a review step anywhere in a workflow. Reviewers get a queue, a UI, and an audit trail.

F.05
Observability

Per-run logs, token costs, latency, eval scores. Alerting on regressions, not vibes.

F.06
Multi-tenant

Each product or engagement is a tenant. Shared platform, isolated data.

PRODUCT 02.2 · AKA

Aptiveon Knowledge Assistant

A practical assistant that helps organizations search, summarize, and act on their own documents, policies, manuals, and internal knowledge.

PRODUCT PILOT DEPLOYMENTS TENANT OF AWE
SPEC SHEET
InputsUploaded docs, structured KBs, role context
OutputsGrounded answers, drafts, cited summaries
StackAWE · LLM providers · vector store
MaturityPilot deployments
Engagement4–8 weeks to first deployment
F.01
Grounded answers

Every answer cites the document and section it came from. Hallucination is visible, not implicit.

F.02
Document ingestion

Drop in PDFs, Office docs, Markdown, Notion exports. Chunking and embeddings handled.

F.03
Configurable prompts

Tune the assistant's voice, refusal behaviour, and scope per deployment without touching code.

F.04
Guardrails

Topic filters, PII handling, and explicit "I don't know" behaviour for out-of-corpus questions.

F.05
Simple UI

A clean chat surface for non-technical teams. No prompt engineering required at the user end.

F.06
Eval harness

Curate a question set, watch coverage and accuracy over time. Catches regressions on doc updates.
