NorthArc Capital capital markets CASE STUDY · CAPITAL MARKETS · SINGAPORE / ZURICH / LONDON

2026.Q1 12-week build · ongoing operate
SectorCapital markets — structured products
Size1,200 staff · 3 jurisdictions
EngagementRAG + Workflow Automation
StackAWE · Knowledge Assistant tenant
RegionSG · CH · UK
Live since2026.02
PROBLEM

Three analysts. Five days. One prospectus.

NorthArc's compliance engineering team reviewed every structured-note prospectus before issuance. Three senior analysts spent a full week on each one, cross-referencing risk factors, term sheets, and disclosures against an internal precedent library that lived across six SharePoint sites and one paralegal's brain.

APPROACH

A reviewer queue, with citations.

We built a workflow on AWE that ingests new prospectuses, runs a multi-step review against the precedent corpus, and surfaces flagged clauses to a human reviewer queue. Every flag points to the source paragraph. Reviewers approve, override, or escalate — and the audit trail is per-run.

OUTCOME

Under four hours to a defensible read.

The workflow now handles the first pass on 312 documents per quarter. Analysts spend their time on the 8–12% that get flagged — the rest passes through with a complete citation trail that satisfies internal audit and the MAS examiner who looked at it in March.

Three analysts spent a week on each prospectus. We get to a defensible read in under four hours — and every flag points to the paragraph it came from.
Priya Raman · Head of Compliance Engineering, NorthArc Capital

Brackmoor Health healthcare CASE STUDY · HOSPITAL NETWORK · UNITED KINGDOM

2026.Q1 16-week build · in operation
SectorHospital network — 7 sites
Size4,800 clinical staff
EngagementAI Digital Assistant + ASD
StackAWE · on-prem deployment
RegionUK · NHS-aligned
Live since2026.03
PROBLEM

SOPs that nobody could find at 2am.

Brackmoor had 1,400 standard operating procedures across seven hospitals — version-stamped PDFs in a portal nobody loved. When a registrar needed the right procedure at 2am, the answer was usually "ask the senior on call." Compliance hated it. Clinicians hated it. Patients didn't know about it.

APPROACH

A grounded assistant, on the ward tablet.

We deployed the Knowledge Assistant against the SOP corpus, with strict refusal behaviour for out-of-corpus questions and explicit "see the senior on call" routing for clinical decisions. Every answer cites the SOP and section number. The whole thing runs on-prem inside Brackmoor's tenancy.

OUTCOME

The right paragraph, in seconds.

Median time from question to cited paragraph: 11 seconds. Clinical Governance signed it off after a four-week shadow audit. The assistant doesn't make clinical decisions — it surfaces the policy and the citation, and clinicians keep doing their jobs.

Our registrars stopped asking each other where the SOP was. They ask the assistant, get the cited paragraph, and move on.
Dr. Iwan Hollis · Director of Clinical Governance, Brackmoor Health

Meridian/H public infra CASE STUDY · PUBLIC INFRASTRUCTURE · NETHERLANDS

2025.Q4 14-week build · operating
SectorPublic infrastructure — water
Size900 staff · 11 facilities
EngagementWorkflow Automation + ASD
StackAWE · self-hosted
RegionEU · NL
Live since2026.01
PROBLEM

Permit applications that took six weeks.

Meridian/H processes ~6,000 industrial discharge permit applications per year. Each one cross-references regulatory limits, historical readings from monitoring stations, and case-law precedents. The median permit sat in the queue for six weeks. The backlog was political.

APPROACH

Automate the easy 80%, route the hard 20%.

We built a workflow that triages incoming applications: clear approvals get a draft response and citations within an hour, complex ones get routed to the senior reviewer queue with a pre-assembled brief. Every routing decision is auditable; reviewers can override.

OUTCOME

From six weeks to nine days.

Median permit time fell from 42 days to 9. Three reviewers retrained onto the harder cases — none laid off. The Inspectorate audited the workflow's audit trail in February and signed off.

The workflow tells me why it routed an application here. I can see the precedent, the readings, and what the model thinks. Then I decide.
Saskia Bekker · Senior Permit Reviewer, Meridian/H

Halden & Lo law CASE STUDY · LAW FIRM · SINGAPORE / HONG KONG

2025.Q3 10-week build · ongoing
SectorLaw — corporate / arbitration
Size180 lawyers · 2 offices
EngagementRAG Knowledge Systems
StackAWE · in-firm tenant
RegionSG · HK
Live since2025.10
PROBLEM

Knowledge that lived in the senior partners.

Halden & Lo's institutional memory — the precedents, the negotiating positions, the way a particular judge tended to rule — sat in the heads of seven senior partners. Associates couldn't access it. Clients paid for it via partner hours. The firm was the bottleneck on its own knowledge.

APPROACH

A grounded research surface.

We built a retrieval system over 18 years of firm work-product — pleadings, advice memos, win/loss notes — with strict client-matter scoping and citation. Associates can ask for "how did we frame this in 2019" and get back the matter, the section, and the partner who drafted it.

OUTCOME

Associates ship draft memos faster.

Time from research request to first-draft memo dropped 64%. Partners review faster because the citations are already there. The data never leaves the firm's tenancy; the model never trains on it.

It surfaces the 2019 memo I forgot I'd written. Three lines of citation. That's the entire game.
Tessa Halden · Senior Partner, Halden & Lo
